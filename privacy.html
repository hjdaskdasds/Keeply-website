Keeply / Legal

Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: September 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 22, 2026

Keeply is designed around local photo processing. The current App does not require an account and does not upload your photo library to a Keeply-operated photo-processing server. Photo analysis and review data are primarily stored on your iPhone.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Keeply is operated by the publisher identified in the applicable App Store listing. For privacy questions or requests, contact matt1232026@gmail.com or use Help & feedback inside Keeply.

If your local law requires a formal data-controller name or address, the publisher and contact details shown in the App Store listing are the controlling identification for the App.

2. Information we collect

The information collected depends on the permissions you grant and the features you use.

2.1 Photo library information

When you grant access to Apple Photos, Keeply may read information needed to scan, classify, compare, and display the items you make available. This can include Apple Photos local asset identifiers; media type, creation date, duration, pixel dimensions, and other technical metadata; on-device image representations, feature descriptors, fingerprints, and quality signals; scan progress, groups of visually similar items, review decisions, saved-for-later items, and cleanup history; and thumbnails or image content temporarily loaded into memory or local storage to display the review interface.

Keeply’s current photo-analysis workflow is performed on the device using Apple frameworks, including Vision. The App is not designed to transmit your photo pixels or photo library to Keeply’s servers. Keeply does not use your photos to build an advertising audience or sell them to third parties.

Apple Photos and iCloud Photos remain separate services. Apple may process your library under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

2.2 Device and app information stored locally

Keeply stores certain preferences and operational information on your iPhone, which may include onboarding status, review order, daily batch size, reminder settings, local scan caches, feature data, review decisions, pending actions, cleanup counts, statistics, app version, and operating-system version.

This information is generally stored using iOS local storage and the App’s local database. It is not automatically an account that we can access remotely.

2.3 Purchase and subscription information

Keeply uses Apple’s App Store and StoreKit to sell products and RevenueCat to retrieve offerings, validate transactions, restore purchases, and maintain entitlement status. Depending on the transaction and provider, the information processed may include an anonymous RevenueCat App User ID; product identifier, price, currency, purchase date, original purchase date, renewal status, expiration date, and entitlement status; receipt or transaction identifiers; App Store country or storefront information; and technical information made available by Apple or RevenueCat.

The current Keeply implementation does not ask for an email address or create a Keeply account before purchase, and it does not intentionally pass your photo content to RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment-card number. Apple processes the payment, and Apple’s and RevenueCat’s own privacy practices also apply.

2.4 Support and communications

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, message, attachments, device/app details that you choose to provide, and the information needed to respond. Please do not send your entire photo library or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

2.5 Notifications and permissions

Keeply may process your reminder preferences and notification authorization state so it can schedule or remove local reminders. The App does not need access to your contacts, microphone, camera, location, health data, or address book for its current core features.

2.6 Diagnostics and analytics

The current Keeply version does not include a separate advertising SDK or a separate third-party behavioral analytics SDK. Apple, RevenueCat, or the operating system may independently collect technical, security, crash, or purchase information under their own policies. If we add analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other data-collection services, we will update this Privacy Policy and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures.

3. How we use information

  1. Provide the App. To scan the photo items you authorize, calculate on-device recommendations, show review screens, and carry out actions you confirm.
  2. Protect your choices. To respect full, limited, or denied Photos access and avoid acting on items outside the scope you grant.
  3. Provide paid features. To display products, complete purchases through Apple, verify entitlements, restore purchases, and show subscription status.
  4. Operate reminders. To schedule local reminders that you request.
  5. Provide support. To answer questions, diagnose issues, and respond to requests.
  6. Maintain security and reliability. To prevent abuse, resolve failures, comply with legal obligations, and protect the App.
  7. Improve Keeply. To understand aggregate product performance and improve features, without using personal photo content for advertising.

4. When information is shared

4.1 Apple

Apple provides Photos, iOS, StoreKit, App Store billing, subscription management, notification infrastructure, and related services. When you grant photo access or make an App Store purchase, Apple processes information under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

4.2 RevenueCat

RevenueCat receives the information needed to retrieve offerings, process purchase events, validate entitlements, restore purchases, and provide purchase-related infrastructure. This can include an anonymous App User ID, purchase history, product identifiers, entitlement status, and related technical information. RevenueCat does not receive Keeply’s local photo content from the current App implementation.

4.3 Service providers and professional advisers

We may disclose limited information to contractors, communications providers, professional advisers, auditors, or security providers that process it for us under confidentiality and security obligations. We do not authorize service providers to use your photo library for their own advertising.

4.4 Legal, safety, and business transfers

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our Terms, investigate fraud or security incidents, protect users or the public, or protect our rights. If Keeply is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be transferred subject to applicable law and appropriate protections.

4.5 No sale of photos; no cross-app advertising

We do not sell your photos or use your photo content for cross-app behavioral advertising. The current Keeply implementation does not request Apple’s advertising identifier and does not operate an advertising SDK.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we generally process information under one or more of these bases: contract, when needed to provide Keeply; consent, when you grant Photos or notification permission; legitimate interests, to secure, maintain, support, and improve the App; and legal obligations, such as accounting, consumer-protection, security, and tax requirements.

You may withdraw a device permission in iPhone Settings, but withdrawal may prevent the related feature from working. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already occurred lawfully before withdrawal.

6. Retention and deletion

6.1 Local photo and review data

Photo identifiers, scan results, local feature data, review decisions, and cleanup history are stored on your device for as long as needed to provide the App or until you remove them. You can stop access by changing Photos permission, remove local app data by deleting Keeply, or use in-App cleanup controls where available. Deleting Keeply does not delete original photos in Apple Photos.

Because the current App does not upload your photo library to a Keeply-operated photo server, we generally do not have a remote copy of your photo analysis to delete.

6.2 Purchase records

Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase, transaction, entitlement, and fraud-prevention records according to their own policies and applicable legal, accounting, and operational requirements. Deleting Keeply does not erase Apple’s purchase history or cancel a subscription.

6.3 Support records

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve disputes, maintain security, satisfy legal obligations, and improve support. You may request deletion of support correspondence subject to legal and operational exceptions.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

To submit a privacy request, email matt1232026@gmail.com with the subject “Keeply Privacy Request.” Tell us what you are requesting and provide enough information for us to respond safely. We may need to verify the request and may decline or limit it where permitted by law.

Keeply-specific choices include changing Photos access in iPhone Settings, disabling reminders, deleting Keeply and its local data, avoiding optional support information, and managing subscriptions through your Apple Account.

8. Children’s privacy

Keeply is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact matt1232026@gmail.com so we can review and delete it where required.

9. International processing

Apple, RevenueCat, and other providers may process information in countries outside your place of residence. Those providers use their own safeguards and transfer mechanisms where required by law. Your applicable rights and protections continue to apply.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the information we process. On-device processing reduces the need to transfer photo content, but no device, app, network, or storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining a passcode and appropriate security controls on your iPhone and Apple Account.

11. Third-party services and links

Keeply may link to Apple subscription pages, RevenueCat-supported purchase functions, or other third-party pages. Those services are not controlled by this Privacy Policy. Review the privacy policies of Apple, RevenueCat, your email provider, and any other service before using them.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, data practices, providers, or law changes. We will publish the revised version at this public legal-policy URL and update the “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects your rights, we may also provide notice in the App or through another reasonable method.
